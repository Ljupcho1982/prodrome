## LinkedIn (long)

Every health app I've ever used asks the same thing: *predict your body.* Log your data today, tag your triggers, guess what's coming.

But if you live with migraines, an autoimmune flare, POTS, or panic attacks, you already know the cruel part — the warning signs happen **hours before you feel anything**, and by the time you're bad enough to reach for an app, that data is already gone.

So I built **Prodrome** — a flight recorder for your body.

It flips the interaction. You don't predict anything. You press **one button** the moment you feel bad. Prodrome then *rewinds the tape* — it replays the passive signals your phone already has (resting heart rate, sleep, and a 10-second voice note it turns into pitch/pace/pause features) and shows you what quietly drifted from **your own** normal in the 24 hours before. After a few episodes, it learns your personal early-warning fingerprint.

Two things I care about most:
🔒 **100% on-device.** No account, no cloud. Nothing ever leaves your phone — even the voice note is discarded; only three numbers are kept.
🧪 **Actually tested.** The detection engine recovered a planted early-warning signal in **5,000 / 5,000** randomized tests, with the noise correctly rejected.

It's free, open source, and the Android APK is up now. If you or someone you love lives between the disease-specific silos, I'd love your feedback.

👉 Download / scan the QR: https://github.com/Ljupcho1982/prodrome/releases/latest
